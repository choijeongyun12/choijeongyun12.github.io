I'm an undergraduate at Kookmin University (Department of Future Mobility), working on autonomous driving, embedded systems, and automotive software.

My current research looks at platooning for electric trucks: how a convoy can safely reorder itself mid-drive (lane detection, lidar-based gap sensing, and state-machine-driven maneuvers), and how to measure whether a reordering strategy actually improves the convoy's energy efficiency and range, using a CARLA + ROS2 simulation stack and a Python battery-management model.

Research interests
======
- Autonomous driving & vehicle platooning
- Embedded systems
- Automotive software
- Sensor fusion & real-time systems

Activities
